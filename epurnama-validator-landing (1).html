Why choose EPurnama

Three things we take care of every day for delegators.

Redundant infrastructure

Primary and backup nodes run in separate locations, so downtime from a single server failure is avoided.

Slashing protection

Sentry nodes and automated monitoring catch anomalies before they can affect the primary validator.

Transparent commission

Commission changes are always announced to delegators in advance — no sudden adjustments.

Regular performance reports

Monthly uptime and reward summaries so delegators always know exactly how the node is doing.

How to delegate

Four steps, done in a few minutes.

01

Open your wallet

Use a wallet that supports this network and make sure your balance covers the transaction fee.

02

Find EPurnama

Go to the staking menu and search for "EPurnama" in the list of active validators.

03

Enter your delegation amount

Set the amount of tokens you want to delegate, then confirm the transaction in your wallet.

04

Track your rewards

Rewards accrue automatically and can be claimed anytime from your wallet.
